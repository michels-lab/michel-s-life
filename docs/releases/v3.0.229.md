## Sound design — Operación Granada

- Replace repetitive calculator-like two-note alarms with **six original cinematic arrangements**, 24 seconds each, in stereo PCM 22.05 kHz: Velvet Dawn / Alba de terciopelo, Glass Garden / Jardín de cristal, Neon Orbit / Órbita neón, First Light / Primera luz, Inner Pulse / Pulso interior, Celestial Tide / Marea celeste.
- Four evolving chord chapters per composition, warm sustained pads, low fundamentals, melodic resonant bells, diffused delays, acoustic dynamics and separate rhythmic identities. No third-party music, samples or internet at alarm delivery.
- Preserved saved tone IDs, existing alarm schedules, repeat/snooze, native notification permissions, user MP3/WAV/OGG upload and original Pomodoro A+C music. Quick Alarm/default new alarms select First Light rather than generic OS sound; existing saved preferences are retained.
- Windows/WebView synthesized stereo buffers; Android caches newly rendered v3 WAV in private storage to avoid accidentally playing previous v2 snippets. Native overlay uses the chosen soundtrack when genuinely audible, and sends OS notifications through a separate silent channel only then; locked/no-overlay/failed playback retains the original audible notification channel.
- Preview extends to 12 seconds to hear music evolve. This is composition, not a UI-only fix.
